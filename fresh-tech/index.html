01 / OUR DIRECTION

A wider field of technology.

Fresh Tech brings together practical engineering and ambitious research. The near-term work is to create useful, dependable systems. The long-term work is to investigate new capabilities, test ideas rigorously and learn where technology can go next.

Build

Turn ideas into usable products, services and infrastructure.

Research

Explore new methods and capabilities through evidence and experimentation.

Connect

Bring systems, people and disciplines together where it creates real value.

03 / HOW WE WORK

Ambition, grounded in evidence.

We distinguish deployed capabilities from work in progress, research and future concepts. We value clear engineering, measurable evaluation, responsible development, security and honest reporting. Big questions deserve disciplined experiments—not unsupported claims.

Fresh Tech is founder-led by Memphis Arafat. Ara6 is the creator and technology-building identity associated with the initiative. The aim is to grow through capable people and collaboration across disciplines.
