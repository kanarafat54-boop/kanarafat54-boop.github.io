01 / ABOUT

Founder-led today.
Built for a wider future.

I’m building Fresh Tech as a broad technology initiative: a growing body of products, services, infrastructure and research that can work together while serving different needs. The ambition reaches beyond any single website, app or conventional platform.

The name Ara6 is associated with my creator and technology-building identity. Fresh Tech is the wider initiative; its individual products and research areas have their own purposes and development paths. I aim to build a capable team of developers, engineers, researchers and collaborators as the work grows.

I value evidence, practical experimentation, responsible development and transparent progress. I distinguish what is live today from what is being built, researched or envisioned.
